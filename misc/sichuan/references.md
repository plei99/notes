# Papers for the Sichuan University notes — English version

Reference index for Patrick Lei's *Log GLSM and related topics*, Sichuan University summer school, July 2026. Checked against all nine sections of the [English notes](notes.pdf) ([TeX source](notes.tex)). Theorem numbers below refer to those notes, not to the papers.

Publication status checked **16 September 2026**. The 47 published entries previously assembled from publisher exports or other fallback metadata have now been checked against authenticated MathSciNet BibTeX exports. Together with the 16 existing MathSciNet records, all 63 published references have MathSciNet provenance. The original citation keys are retained, with MR identifiers recorded in `references.bib`. Linked titles lead to journal versions where identified. Published proceedings, books, and memoirs are labelled separately. “Preprint” means that I found an arXiv version but did not identify a journal version. Results sharing a source are grouped together; elementary calculations and standard background facts do not each receive a separate research citation.

The remaining source gaps are the Guo–Xu–Zhang results in Section 9 and two precise conjectures in Section 8. The new genus-one quantum K results in Section 5 are credited to You-Cheng Chou without a paper citation. Section 7 also uses an author-hosted working draft that is not on arXiv. The citations are integrated into the English notes, with the entries in [references.bib](references.bib).
