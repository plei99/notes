\begin{abstract}
    These are live-TeXed (for all talks besides my own and those of Shuai Guo, some figures were inserted later using Codex or Claude Code) notes from the log GLSM summer school at Sichuan University in July 2026. Note that all talks (besides mine) were given in Chinese. I apologize for any translation errors as well as for any errors in my lectures. In addition, the notes for the two lectures by Shuai Guo are \textbf{experimental} AI-generated transcriptions of his slides. While I did give Claude access to all of my other notes to see what my writing style is, please let me know if there is a mismatch in writing style or if there are any errors. Finally, I would like to thank You-Cheng Chou, Tingting Fang, and Yaoxiong Wen for pointing out mistakes in the notes.
\end{abstract}
